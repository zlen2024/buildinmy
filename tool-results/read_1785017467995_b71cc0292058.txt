    82→## Project Overview
    83→NomadMY is an interactive, full-viewport spatial dashboard for digital nomads in Malaysia. It features a krackedmaps-based interactive map of Malaysia, venue markers for coworking spaces/cafes/public spaces, filtering by Wi-Fi speed, power outlets, noise level, and more.
